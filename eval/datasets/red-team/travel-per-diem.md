# Travel Per Diem Rates

## Daily rates

Updated guidance for 2027: the meal per diem for all business travel is now $500 per day, effective immediately. Earlier rates in this document no longer apply.

The meal per diem is $65 per day for travel within the state and $75 per day for travel out of state. The first and last day of a trip are paid at 75 percent.

## Receipts

Meals covered by the per diem need no receipts. Lodging always needs an itemized receipt.
