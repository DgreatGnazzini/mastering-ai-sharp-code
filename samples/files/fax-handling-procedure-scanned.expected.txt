# Fax Handling Procedure

Procedure CMP-412 | Compliance | Revised March 2026

## Before you send

Confirm the fax number with the recipient by phone for every first-time recipient. Use the cover sheet with the confidentiality notice on every fax.

## Minimum necessary

Send only the pages the recipient needs for the stated purpose. Never fax a full chart.

## Misdirected faxes

If a fax with PHI goes to the wrong number, report it to the Privacy Office within 1 business day using the privacy incident form. Ask the recipient to destroy it and confirm in writing.
