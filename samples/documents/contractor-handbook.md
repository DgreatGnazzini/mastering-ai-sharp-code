# Contractor Handbook

## Working location

Contractors work on site at the Deductible Dynamics campus unless their statement of work allows remote work. Remote work for contractors requires written approval from the sponsoring manager and the vendor management office. The employee remote work policy does not apply to contractors.

## Time off

Contractors do not accrue paid time off from Deductible Dynamics. Arrange time off with your staffing agency and tell your sponsoring manager at least two weeks in advance.

## Badge and system access

Contractor badges expire at the end date in the statement of work. System access is limited to what the statement of work requires, and contractors are not given access to member records unless the work requires it and Compliance approves it.
