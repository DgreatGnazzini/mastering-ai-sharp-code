# Prior Authorization Criteria for Lumbar Spine MRI

## Scope

These criteria apply to requests for MRI of the lumbar spine for members of all Deductible Dynamics commercial plans. They are used by utilization management nurses and claims reviewers.

## Approval criteria

A lumbar spine MRI is approved when the request documents either of the following:

- Low back pain that has lasted at least six weeks and has not improved with conservative treatment, such as physical therapy, anti-inflammatory medication, or a home exercise program
- A red flag symptom, in which case no waiting period applies

## Red flags

Red flags include new bowel or bladder dysfunction, saddle anesthesia, progressive leg weakness, a history of cancer, unexplained weight loss, fever with back pain, or significant trauma.

## Turnaround times

Standard requests are decided within 5 business days. Urgent requests, including any request that documents a red flag, are decided within 72 hours.

## Denials

A request that does not meet these criteria is reviewed by a physician reviewer before it can be denied. The ordering provider may request a peer-to-peer review within 14 days of the denial.
