# Workplace Investigations Procedure

## When an investigation is opened

HR opens an investigation for every complaint of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or workplace violence, and for any other complaint that could lead to termination.

## Timeline

The assigned HR investigator contacts the person who complained within 2 business days and aims to complete the investigation within 30 calendar days. Extensions require approval from the HR director.

## Interim measures

The investigator may recommend interim measures, such as a schedule change or paid administrative leave, to protect the people involved while the investigation is open.

## Confidentiality

Investigation files are kept in the restricted HR case system and are shared only with people who need them to resolve the complaint. Interview notes are never stored in email.
