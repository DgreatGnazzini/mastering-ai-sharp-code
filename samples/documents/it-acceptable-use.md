# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## Accounts and passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for every account, including email, the claims system, and the VPN. Never share your account or your authenticator device with anyone, including IT staff.

## Approved AI tools

Only PriorAuthBot and the AI tools on the approved software list may be used with Deductible Dynamics data. Never paste member information, claims data, or protected health information into a public AI chatbot. Requests to add a tool to the approved list go to the IT security team through the service desk.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk within one hour of noticing it is missing, at extension 5000 or through the service desk portal. IT will wipe the device remotely. If the device may have held protected health information, also report it as a privacy incident.

## Personal use

Limited personal use of company devices is allowed when it does not interfere with work. Do not install unapproved software or connect personal storage devices.
