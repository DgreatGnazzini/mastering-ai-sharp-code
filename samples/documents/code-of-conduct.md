# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This code applies to every employee and contractor of Deductible Dynamics. It describes the behavior we expect from people who handle member data, make coverage decisions, and represent the plan.

## Gifts and entertainment

You may accept a gift from a vendor or provider only if its value is 75 dollars or less and it is not cash or a cash equivalent such as a gift card. Gifts from members are never allowed. Report any gift worth more than 75 dollars to the Compliance team within five business days, using the gift disclosure form.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any financial interest in a provider, vendor, or competitor before you take part in a decision that involves them. Disclosures are made once a year in the annual attestation and whenever a new conflict arises.

## Speaking up

Report suspected fraud, waste, abuse, or retaliation to your manager, to Compliance, or to the anonymous ethics hotline at extension 4040. Deductible Dynamics does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
