# Sepsis Screening Protocol

## Scope

This protocol applies to nurses and clinicians in Deductible Dynamics urgent care clinics. It is a screening aid and does not replace clinical judgment.

## Screening criteria

Screen every adult patient who presents with a suspected infection. The screen is positive when the patient has two or more of the following:

- Temperature above 38.3 C or below 36.0 C
- Heart rate above 90 beats per minute
- Respiratory rate above 20 breaths per minute
- New confusion or altered mental status
- Systolic blood pressure at or below 100 mmHg

## Escalation

A positive screen must be escalated to the supervising physician within 15 minutes. Draw a lactate level and blood cultures before starting antibiotics, unless drawing them would delay antibiotics by more than 45 minutes. If the systolic blood pressure stays below 90 mmHg after initial fluids, call emergency services for transfer to the nearest emergency department.

## Documentation

Record the screen result, the time of escalation, and the name of the physician notified in the clinic record. Positive screens are reviewed weekly by the clinical quality team.
