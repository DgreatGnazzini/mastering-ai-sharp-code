# Paid Time Off Policy

## Eligibility

All regular full-time and part-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) from their first day. Temporary employees and contractors do not accrue PTO under this policy.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO each pay period based on years of service:

- Less than 3 years of service: 6.15 hours per pay period, or 20 days a year
- 3 to 9 years of service: 7.69 hours per pay period, or 25 days a year
- 10 or more years of service: 9.23 hours per pay period, or 30 days a year

Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover

You may carry over up to 40 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Hours above 40 are forfeited on December 31 unless your manager approved an exception in writing before December 1.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more. Requests for a single day can be made with one day of notice. Managers approve or decline requests within three business days.

## Payout at separation

Unused accrued PTO, up to the 40-hour carryover limit, is paid out in the final paycheck when an employee leaves Deductible Dynamics.
