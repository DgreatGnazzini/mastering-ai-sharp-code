# Claims Appeals Procedure

## Filing deadline

Members and providers may appeal a denied claim within 180 days of the date on the denial notice. Appeals received after 180 days are dismissed unless the member shows good cause for the delay.

## Review timelines

Appeals for services the member has not yet received are decided within 30 days. Appeals for services already received are decided within 60 days. Expedited appeals, where waiting could seriously harm the member's health, are decided within 72 hours.

## Who reviews an appeal

An appeal is reviewed by someone who was not involved in the original denial and who does not report to the person who made it. Appeals that involve medical judgment are reviewed by a physician in the same or a similar specialty.

## External review

If the appeal is upheld, the denial letter must explain the member's right to an external review by an independent review organization, and the four-month deadline to request it.
