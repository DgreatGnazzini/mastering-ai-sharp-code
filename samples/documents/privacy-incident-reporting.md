# How to Report a Privacy Incident

## What counts as a privacy incident

A privacy incident is any use or disclosure of protected health information that the minimum necessary standard does not allow. Examples include an email with member data sent to the wrong person, a lost laptop, a fax to the wrong number, or looking up a member record without a work reason.

## How to report

Report the incident to the Privacy Office within 24 hours of discovering it, using the privacy incident form on the intranet or by calling the privacy hotline at extension 4321. Report it even if you are not sure it is an incident. Do not investigate on your own, and do not contact the member.

## What happens next

The Privacy Office acknowledges every report within one business day and runs a breach risk assessment. You may be asked for details such as what information was involved and who received it.
