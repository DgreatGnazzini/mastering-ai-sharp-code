# HIPAA Breach Risk Assessment Procedure

## Purpose

The Privacy Office uses this procedure to decide whether a privacy incident is a reportable breach of unsecured protected health information.

## The four-factor assessment

For each incident, the privacy officer documents four factors:

1. The nature and extent of the protected health information involved, including identifiers and the likelihood of re-identification
2. The unauthorized person who used the information or to whom it was disclosed
3. Whether the information was actually acquired or viewed
4. The extent to which the risk has been mitigated

## Notification deadlines

If the assessment cannot show a low probability that the information was compromised, the incident is a breach. Affected members must be notified without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. Breaches affecting 500 or more residents of a state also require notice to prominent media outlets and to the Secretary of Health and Human Services within the same 60 days.

## Records

Keep every assessment, including those that conclude there was no breach, for six years.
