# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees in roles marked hybrid-eligible may work remotely up to three days a week after completing their 90-day introductory period. Clinical staff who see members in person are not eligible for remote work on scheduled clinic days.

## Equipment and security

Remote work must be done on a Deductible Dynamics laptop connected through the VPN. Protected health information may not be printed at home. Work in a private space where others cannot see your screen or hear calls about members.

## Home office stipend

Hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 300 dollars, claimed through the expense reimbursement process within 60 days of their remote work start date.
