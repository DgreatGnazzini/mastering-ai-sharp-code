# Medication Reconciliation Procedure

## When to reconcile

Reconcile medications at every clinic visit, at every transition of care, and within 48 hours of a member's discharge from a hospital.

## Steps

1. Collect the member's current medication list from at least two sources, such as the member, the pharmacy claims history, and the discharge summary.
2. Compare the list with the medications ordered at this visit.
3. Resolve every discrepancy with the prescribing clinician before the member leaves.
4. Give the member an updated medication list in plain language.

## High-alert medications

Anticoagulants, insulin, and opioids require a second clinician to verify the reconciled list. Document the verifier's name in the record.
