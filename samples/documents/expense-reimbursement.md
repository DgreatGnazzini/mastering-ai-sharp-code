# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting expenses

Submit business expenses in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt for any expense over 25 dollars. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Mileage

Business travel in a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 70 cents per mile. Commuting between home and your regular work location is not reimbursable.

## Meals

Meals during approved business travel are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per day. Alcohol is never reimbursed.
